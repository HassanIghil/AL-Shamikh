# Local SEO checklist

## Confirm eligibility and operating facts before creating a profile

- [ ] Confirm in-person customer contact during stated hours, such as a real customer-facing location, visits or direct delivery.
- [ ] Confirm the real business name, owner/authorized representative, phone, hours, signage and operating model.
- [ ] Do not invent a Jeddah or Riyadh office. Service-area businesses should use one profile for the actual area served, not a profile per target city.
- [ ] If customers are not served at an address, do not publish that address; confirm current profile policy for the operating model.
- [ ] Select categories based on actual business activities, not target phrases.
- [ ] Set only service areas the team actually covers.
- [ ] Upload genuine current product, workplace and project photos with permission; clearly label illustrative images on the site.
- [ ] Keep business name, phone and official website consistent on legitimate listings.
- [ ] Request honest feedback from real customers without rewards; never fabricate reviews or ratings.
- [ ] Recheck business details after operational changes.

## If eligible and verified

- [ ] Owner claims and verifies the profile.
- [ ] Complete categories, service model, hours, contact and accurate service areas.
- [ ] Add the official domain only after launch and test it.
- [ ] Measure profile clicks/calls separately from organic website traffic.
- [ ] Keep management under the owner or authorized representative.

Google's guidance requires in-person customer contact for eligibility. Local results are mainly associated with relevance, distance and prominence; no one can pay or request a better local rank. Service-area profiles should reflect actual service and a single profile may cover the actual service area.
