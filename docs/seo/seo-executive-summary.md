## Fastest credible path to qualified inquiries

1. Obtain the official domain, service-area truth, installation/supply scope, verified products/specifications, real images and consent, and contact confirmation.
2. Keep the current preview noindex. At domain launch, validate canonical host, robots, sitemap and Search Console before opening indexing.
3. Strengthen four existing commercial pages: /warehouse-racking, /jeddah, /riyadh, /retail-shelving. Clearly state what is available, how selection works, what project details/photos help, and the next step on WhatsApp, using client-approved facts.
4. Replace illustrative imagery with real documented work only after the client supplies and approves it.
5. Measure organic visits, WhatsApp outbound clicks and qualified inquiries separately.
6. Expand to standalone system pages only after GSC/lead evidence and a distinct, verified service page brief.

## Needed from client

Owned domain and canonical host; real business/profile eligibility details; confirmed city coverage and delivery/installation commitments; product inventory and approved technical specs; genuine project photos and publication permissions; verified telephone/contact facts; English-sales capability; GSC/Analytics/Ads access.

## Evidence and limitations

Existing source datasets and the new variant snapshot are linked in competitor-analysis.md. Monthly volume, CPC, difficulty, Trends, client rank, traffic and conversion metrics are unknown.
