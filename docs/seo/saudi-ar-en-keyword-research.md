## Competitors and content gaps

In the retained 14-query panel, RFCO appeared in 8 organic query result sets (6 top-three appearances), Sadr in 5 (3 top-three), Krfof in 4 (3 top-three), and Alesayi ranked first in both sampled English warehouse queries. Haraj and social platforms also appeared repeatedly. These counts describe that panel only. [Sadr’s Jeddah warehouse article](https://www.sadr.com.sa/en/blog/Jeddah-warehouse-shelves) illustrates a city-specific informational article; [RFCO’s storage category](https://rfcosa.com/ar/%D8%B1%D9%81%D9%88%D9%81-%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B2%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85/c201713318) emphasizes catalog browsing; [Alesayi Storage](https://alesayistorage.com/) exposes more detailed system navigation. These are observed page patterns, not evidence of competitor traffic or conversion.

Al Shamikh can differentiate with accurate selection inputs, clear city coverage, understandable warehouse-versus-retail paths, and authorized completed-project photographs when the owner supplies project facts. The existing copy already does much of this. Technical system specifications, capacity tables, installation promises, addresses, warranties and project outcomes need owner evidence before publication.

## Changes made in this phase and next steps

1. Added the natural synonym **رفوف المخازن** to the warehouse meta description.
2. Added confirmed **صيدليات** to the Arabic retail meta description.
3. Added **warehouse racking** to the English warehouse title. The existing H1s, useful service descriptions, FAQs, internal links, city coverage, and image labels already fit their distinct intents; they were retained.
4. After launch, use verified Search Console query/page pairs to detect cannibalization and decide which wording deserves expansion. Request Keyword Planner or an approved metrics batch if volume is needed. Do not infer volume from SERP presence or Google Trends relative interest.

## Remaining data and cost gates

OpenSEO live Google SERPs are approximately 5 credits per query at depth 20; seed keyword discovery is approximately 54 credits per seed, beyond the currently reported 23-credit balance. The official production domain is not configured as the listed OpenSEO project. Any paid call or official-domain audit needs a separate scoped approval.
