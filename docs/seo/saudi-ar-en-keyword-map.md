2. **Ready when factual assets arrive:** Add real completed-project photos with city, type, category, consent and a short factual description. Until then, illustrative labels stay.
3. **Requires owner confirmation:** Publish technical rack types, capacities and installation scope only with product documentation.
4. **Requires measurement:** Review Search Console queries, country, page, impressions and clicks after indexing; investigate overlapping city and specialist URLs before changing their targets.
5. **Requires separate approval:** Consider a pharmacy or pallet-racking specialist page only after distinct demand and unique verified product detail are demonstrated.
