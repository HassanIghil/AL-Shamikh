# 90-day content and measurement roadmap

The schedule begins after the official domain is ready for indexing. Keep pages.dev noindex. Client-dependent work can be prepared earlier, but do not add unsupported facts.

## Days 0–30: launch and evidence

- Confirm official domain, canonical hostname, business identity, service areas, supply/install scope and approved genuine photos.
- Verify production metadata, static HTML, HTTPS, redirects, canonicals, robots, sitemap and deployed 404.
- Verify Search Console domain property and submit sitemap only after indexing approval.
- Define measurement for organic landing pages, outbound WhatsApp clicks, qualified inquiries and sales separately. A click is not a sent message.
- Improve /warehouse-racking, /jeddah and /riyadh with confirmed process, quote inputs (dimensions, layout/photos, product type, city) and natural contextual links.
- Capture baseline impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, indexed pages, mobile performance and lead outcomes; do not fabricate targets.

## Days 31–60: strengthen usefulness and trust

- Improve /retail-shelving and /sectors with confirmed supermarket, grocery, pharmacy and shop-fixture details.
- Add buyer answers from sales staff, with engineering claims only after technical approval.
- Publish genuine project cases only with client photos, consent, actual scope and verified location.
- Review GSC queries/pages for intent mismatch, overlap and actual city/service coverage.
- QA WhatsApp CTA events and manually reconcile qualified leads with the business owner.

## Days 61–90: evidence-led expansion

- Review 6–8 weeks of GSC and lead data; note low-volume limits and seasonality.
- Evaluate pallet, pharmacy, supermarket or installation pages only if demand/coverage, confirmed service, original assets and differentiated content all support them.
- Test English content only if sales can support English enquiries.
- Repeat the same SERP sample with matching location/device/language settings and preserved timestamps.
- Decide keep/improve/expand for each page; report observed outcomes, no rank promises.

Measures are reporting indicators, not guaranteed improvements. Google notes search results vary and changes may take time; do not promise a top-three or #1 position.
